# EcoNode Demo Video Script

## Suggested Flow
- 0:00-0:10: Show the EcoNode banner and introduce the problem: wasted cloud resources cost money and energy.
- 0:10-0:30: Show the agent analyzing AWS resources and highlighting a low-usage or idle instance.
- 0:30-0:50: Highlight the savings estimate, risk score, and recommended action in the Slack message.
- 0:50-1:10: Demonstrate the human approval workflow by replying with an approval command in Slack.
- 1:10-1:30: Show the audit log and confirm the action was logged and tracked.
- 1:30-1:50: End with the impact statement: less waste, safer operations, measurable savings.

## Talking Points
- EcoNode helps teams act faster without removing human oversight.
- It focuses on environmental sustainability by reducing unnecessary cloud usage.
- The workflow is transparent, auditable, and easy to use inside Slack.
